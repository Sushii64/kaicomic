Your inventory is actually pretty simple.

Items you pick up turn into cards. This is called CATASTRACTING ITEMS onto CATASTRACT CARDS. Your INVENTORY is your THINGBANK. This is how you carry items with you, because using a BAG is lame.

Your DRAWPROC is the means by which your THINGBANK actually functions. For example, a DEFAULT PROC means limited slots, but you can easily store and pull from any slot, any time.

Yours is a bit different.

As mentioned before, you use the WHERE’S WALDO® PROC. You have infinite slots, but there’s a catch, because easy things don’t just come for free.

Catastracted cards get randomly placed as pictures on a simple Where’s Waldo map. To retrieve said card, you simply have to find the picture of the item. This will pull the card out of your thingbank and give you the item.

You like this proc. It’s adventury.
