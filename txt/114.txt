You are narratively bound to not yet talk about the first part of the story in much detail, so alas, you will refrain.

Essentially, there were once four great kings of the land. They each had their own land. The fourth one’s land was completely nonsensical, and their subjects spilled out into the others’ lands. The other kings, naturally, did not like this. They exiled the fourth king using very powerful magic, and they were never seen again.

Where you come in is when the first king got greedy. He wanted more. He trained his subjects to resent the other two lands, enough to bring them to war.
