The Device.

That is to say, the Void Gateway Instigator Device. It sort of kind of pokes a hole into the fabric of space itself? It’s a little hard to explain, you don’t quite know the specifics of it.

Once the hole is poked, it grabs a hold of it and tears it apart until it’s large enough to enter. Once that’s done, and someone enters, you close the portal by patching it up.

Simple stuff, really.
